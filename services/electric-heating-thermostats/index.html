Behind The Job

Sizing Electric Heating to the Room, Not the Nearest Heater

The most common issue we find with electric heating isn't the heater itself — it's an undersized circuit trying to run it, often because it was plugged into a general socket circuit rather than given a dedicated supply. We calculate the actual load a room's heating will draw before deciding on cable size and circuit protection.

Garden rooms and outbuildings need particular thought, since the supply often has to run some distance from the main property, buried or ducted correctly along the way. Getting the cable sizing and protection right at that stage avoids voltage drop and nuisance tripping once the heating's actually in use.

FAQs

Common Questions About Electric Heating & Thermostats

Will this need a new circuit from the consumer unit?
In most cases yes, sized specifically to the heating load — we'll confirm this and any consumer unit capacity implications during the quote.
Can you replace old storage heaters?
Yes, including removing the old units and wiring in modern, more controllable panel heaters or radiators in their place.
Is electric heating expensive to run compared to gas?
Per unit of energy it's generally more expensive than gas, but for smaller spaces, garden rooms, or properties without a gas connection, a properly sized and controlled system can still be the most practical option.
Do panel heaters need their own circuit?
For anything beyond a single small heater, yes — running heating from a general socket circuit risks overloading it, which is why we size a dedicated circuit to the heater's actual demand.
Can you fit and set up a Hive thermostat?
Yes, including the electrical wiring and the app configuration, so it's genuinely ready to use when we leave, not just physically installed.
Can you wire heating into a garden room or outbuilding?
Yes, this is a common job for us — including running the supply out from the main property if that's needed.
How do I know what size heater a room needs?
It depends on room size, insulation, and how it's used — we'll size this properly during the quote rather than fitting whatever's convenient.
