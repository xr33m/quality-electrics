EV & Fleet Charging Installation in Glasgow

Home EV chargers through to fleet and forklift charging infrastructure for commercial sites.

EV charging covers a wider range than most people expect — a single home charger is a genuinely different job to load-managed charging infrastructure for a multi-vehicle fleet or a warehouse forklift fleet. We handle both ends of that range, sized properly to what's actually needed.

Supply capacity is the thing that actually determines what's possible, so every EV charging job starts with an honest look at what your existing supply can handle before we recommend a specific setup.

Common Questions

EV & Fleet Charging Installation FAQs

Do you install home EV chargers as well as commercial fleet charging?

Yes, from a single home charger through to load-managed multi-point fleet and forklift charging for commercial and industrial sites.

What determines whether a supply upgrade is needed?

Your existing supply capacity versus the number and type of charge points wanted — we assess this honestly before recommending a specific installation, including load management as an alternative to a full upgrade.

Can EV charging be added to an existing consumer unit or distribution board?

Often yes for a single home charger, provided there's capacity — larger installations may need a dedicated circuit or supply assessment, which we'll confirm before quoting.
